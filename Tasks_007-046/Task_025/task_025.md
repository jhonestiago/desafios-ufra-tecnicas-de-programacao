# Task 25

## PT

Faça um algoritmo/programa que receba duas notas de um aluno e seus respectivos pesos, calcule e mostre a média ponderada dessas notas.

$$
mediaPonderada = \frac{(nota1 * peso1) + (nota2 * peso2)}{(peso1 + peso2)}
$$

## EN

Create an algorithm/program that receives two grades of a student and their respective weights, calculates and shows the weighted average of these grades.

$$
weightedAverage = \frac{(grade1 * weight1) + (grade2 * weight2)}{(weight1 + weight2)}
$$
